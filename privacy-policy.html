VitePulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026  |  Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Indosat ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects your personal data when you use the VitePulse mobile application (the "App"). VitePulse lets you connect your HUAWEI Health account so the App can read health and fitness data recorded by your HUAWEI watch or wearable and show it to you in one place.

By using the App and choosing to connect HUAWEI Health, you confirm that you have read this Policy. Health data is only read after you give explicit consent.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Health data from HUAWEI Health (only with your consent)

When you connect HUAWEI Health through HUAWEI Health Service Kit, you choose which data types to share on the HUAWEI authorization screen. The App may request read access to the following:

Data typeWhy we use it
Step countShow your daily and weekly steps and progress toward your goal.
DistanceShow distance covered in your activity history.
Calories burnedShow estimated daily calories burned on your dashboard.
Heart rateShow your heart rate readings and trend in your health summary.
SleepShow sleep duration and your sleep trend.
Workout recordsList your past workouts (type, duration) in your activity log.

The App only reads this data. It does not write to or change your HUAWEI Health records. We do not request any health data that is not needed for the features above.

2.2 Account and contact data

If you create an account: name or nickname, email address, and account credentials (passwords are stored in hashed form).

2.3 Device and usage data

Device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash logs and basic usage events (such as screens viewed), used to keep the App working and to fix bugs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your health and activity data, trends and goals inside the App.
  • To create and manage your account and provide support.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix errors.
  • To improve the App using aggregated or de-identified information.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
We do not sell your data. We do not use your health data for advertising, marketing profiling, or to determine insurance eligibility, pricing, credit or employment decisions.

4. Legal Basis

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent (especially for health data), the need to provide the services you requested, our legitimate interests in keeping the App secure, and legal obligations, in accordance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and other applicable regulations.

5. Sharing of Data

We share personal data only in these limited cases:

  • Huawei: authorization and data access run through HUAWEI Health and HUAWEI Health Service Kit. HUAWEI handles your HUAWEI account data under its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers: hosting, cloud storage and crash-reporting providers that process data on our behalf under confidentiality and security obligations: [e.g. cloud provider name, region].
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a valid request from authorities.
  • Business transfer: if we merge or are acquired, and only under the same protections as this Policy.

We do not share your health data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Storage, Retention and Transfers

  • Data is stored on servers located in [Jakarta / Singapore] and, where applicable, on your device.
  • We keep health data only as long as your account is active and you keep HUAWEI Health connected. If you withdraw consent or delete your account, we delete or anonymize your data within [30] days, unless the law requires us to keep certain records longer.
  • If data is transferred outside Indonesia, we ensure an adequate level of protection as required by law.

7. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, role-based access controls, restricted staff access, and regular security reviews. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a data breach as required by law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data and your account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already done.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

How to withdraw HUAWEI Health access: open the App > Settings > Connected Services > Disconnect HUAWEI Health, or revoke authorization in HUAWEI Health settings (Me > Privacy > Data sharing / Authorization management; menu names may vary by version). After you disconnect, the App stops reading your data.

To exercise other rights, email [privacy@yourdomain.com]. We will respond within a reasonable time and no later than required by law.

9. Children

VitePulse is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Not a Medical Service

VitePulse displays information for general wellness and fitness purposes. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
